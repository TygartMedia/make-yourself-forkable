# What I Will Not Automate

A short list, in public, so I can't quietly move the line later. Everything in this repo argues that judgment is the asset and generated output is exhaust. Here's what stays on my side.

1. **The publish tap.** Nothing ships without a human saying go. Not the article, not the post, not the work order. The gate is the product.

2. **Deciding what belongs in the packet.** The machine can assemble it. Only someone who's been on the call knows what goes in it — and what gets left out. The omission is the judgment.

3. **Overruling the halt.** I will automate the stop: an agent that grades its own work badly should exit on its own. I will not automate the decision that the halt was wrong. Appeals go to a human.

4. **The price.** No agent invents a discount, a term, or a promise on my behalf. Numbers I didn't set don't leave the building.

5. **Other people's names.** No client named, no live vulnerability described, no private conversation quoted — not by me, not by anything acting for me. Redacted nouns or it doesn't publish.

6. **Showing up.** The call, the visit, the remembering. Fewer audiences, more regular genuine humans — and the work there is mine to do, not to delegate.

7. **The why.** Traffic is a side effect. If a piece only earns traffic, it doesn't earn the merge.

This list gets shorter only by public edit, never by quiet drift.
